Part 1 • Transport Systems

1. Simple organism / Unicellular organism

(a) Does a simple organism have a specific transport system?
(b) Name the process of transporting necessary substances and excretory products through the cell membrane.

Answer
(a) No. 华语：没有特定的运输系统。 BM: Tidak.
(b) Diffusion. 华语：扩散。 BM: Resapan.

2. Complex organisms

(a) Does a complex organism have a specific transport system?
(b) Complete how the special transport system works.

Answer
(a) Yes. 华语：有特定运输系统。 BM: Ya.
(b) It transports oxygen and nutrients into all body cells and removes excretory products from the cells out of the body.
华语关键词：氧气、营养物质、排泄物。
BM: oksigen, nutrien, bahan kumuh.

Part 3 • Heartbeat Sequence

Diastole

Semilunar valves: closed.
Tricuspid and bicuspid valves: opened.
Atriums and ventricles: relax.
Sound: “dub”.
Blood flows from the atriums into the ventricles.
Pressure reading: diastolic.
华语：舒张期，心房流向心室，舒张压。

Systole

Semilunar valves: opened.
Tricuspid and bicuspid valves: closed.
Atriums and ventricles: contract.
Sound: “lub”.
Blood flows from the ventricles into the blood vessels.
Pressure reading: systolic.
华语：收缩期，心室把血液泵入血管，收缩压。
