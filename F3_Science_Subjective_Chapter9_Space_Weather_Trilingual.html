Question 6

(a) Sunspots are the dark regions seen on the surface of the Sun. Sunspots occur because their temperature is lower than their surrounding areas.
Tompok Matahari ialah kawasan gelap pada permukaan Matahari. Ia terjadi kerana kawasan itu lebih sejuk dari kawasan sekitarnya.

(b) Solar storms occur due to the phenomena of solar flares on the Sun. Causes disruption to electrical substation system. Spacecraft and satellite operations were disturbed.
Ribut suria berlaku akibat fenomena nyalaan suria pada Matahari. Menyebabkan gangguan terhadap sistem pencawang elektrik. Operasi kapal angkasa dan satelit terganggu.

(c) Predict when coronal mass ejection occur in the Sun. Determines the reason for the occurrence of solar flares and coronal mass ejection on the surface of the Sun.

(d) Solar flares cause the charged particles to be released and formed a solar wind. Part of the plasma on this solar storm will be trapped in the layer of magnetosphere and follows the magnetic field path of the Earth towards the north and south poles. Aurora is formed when cosmic radiation strike atoms and molecules of the Earth's atmosphere.
