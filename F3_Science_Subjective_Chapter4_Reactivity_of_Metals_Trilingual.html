Part 1 • Calcium Compounds

(a) Forms of calcium carbonate

Tick: Coral reef, Limestone, Shell, Eggshell, Animal bone.
华语：珊瑚礁、石灰石、贝壳、蛋壳、动物骨骼。
BM: Terumbu karang, Batu kapur, Siput, Cangkerang telur, Tulang haiwan.

(b) Gas released when acid is added to eggshell

Carbon dioxide.
Reason: An eggshell contains calcium carbonate.
华语：释放二氧化碳，因为蛋壳含有碳酸钙。
BM: Karbon dioksida; sebab cangkerang telur mengandungi kalsium karbonat.

(c) Identify calcium compounds according to uses

(a) Calcium oxide
(b) Calcium carbonate
(c) Sand

(d) Calcium carbonate classification

Calcium carbonate is a natural compound which consists of a combination of a few elements.
华语关键词：化合物、元素。
BM: sebatian, unsur.

Part 3 • Extraction of Iron

(a) Elements that make up iron ore

Iron and oxygen.
BM: Besi/Ferum dan oksigen.

(b) K is a type of carbon. Name K.

Coke. BM: Arang kok.

(c) Name L and state its function

L = Limestone / Calcium carbonate.
Function: Reacts with impurities such as sand to form slag.
华语：石灰石／碳酸钙，与砂等杂质反应形成炉渣。

(d) Gas M and substance N

Gas M = Carbon dioxide
Substance N = Slag
BM: Karbon dioksida; Sanga.

(e) Word equation

Iron(III) oxide + Carbon → Iron + Carbon dioxide

(f) Why can iron be extracted using carbon?

Carbon is more reactive than iron.
华语：碳比铁更活泼。
BM: Karbon lebih reaktif daripada ferum.

(g) Uses of molten slag

House foundation and Road foundation.
BM: Tapak rumah, tapak jalan raya.

(h) Another ore extracted by the same method

Tin ore. BM: Bijih timah.
