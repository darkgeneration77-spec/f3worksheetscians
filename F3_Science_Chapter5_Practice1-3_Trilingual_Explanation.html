Practice 1Compare exothermic and endothermic reactions

Task: Compare solid sodium hydroxide dissolved in water with solid potassium nitrate dissolved in water.
ItemSodium hydroxide + waterPotassium nitrate + water
(a) Type of reactionExothermic reactionEndothermic reaction
(b) DefinitionReleases heat into the surroundingsAbsorbs heat from the surroundings
(c) Thermometer readingIncreasesDecreases
(d) ContainerBecomes hotBecomes cold
(e) Energy contentProducts have less total energy than reactantsProducts have higher total energy than reactants

English explanation

Exothermic reactions transfer heat from the reacting system to the surroundings. The thermometer rises and the container feels hot. Endothermic reactions take heat from the surroundings, so the temperature falls and the container feels cold.

华语讲解

放热反应把热量从反应体系释放到周围，温度计读数上升，容器变热。吸热反应从周围吸收热量，温度下降，容器变冷。

Penjelasan BM

Tindak balas eksotermik membebaskan haba daripada sistem tindak balas ke persekitaran. Bacaan suhu meningkat dan bekas menjadi panas. Tindak balas endotermik menyerap haba daripada persekitaran, maka suhu menurun dan bekas menjadi sejuk.
口诀 / Mnemonic / Formula ingatan:
EXO = heat EXits（热出去 / haba keluar）
ENDO = heat ENters（热进入 / haba masuk）
releases heatabsorbs heattemperature increasestemperature decreasessurroundingsenergy content

Answering steps / 作答步骤 / Langkah menjawab

Look at the thermometer: rising temperature = exothermic; falling temperature = endothermic.
State heat direction: system → surroundings for exothermic; surroundings → system for endothermic.
Link to touch: hot container = heat released; cold container = heat absorbed.
Compare energy: exothermic products lower; endothermic products higher.

(d) Heating copper(II) carbonate

Important source discrepancy: The uploaded answer sheet labels this as “Exothermic reaction” but immediately states that copper(II) carbonate “absorbs heat”. Those two statements conflict. According to the heat-direction criterion used throughout the same chapter, a reaction that absorbs heat is endothermic. The source wording has been preserved and the inconsistency is made explicit rather than silently changed.

English explanation

Copper(II) carbonate absorbs heat and decomposes into copper(II) oxide and carbon dioxide. By the chapter definition, absorbing heat indicates an endothermic reaction.

华语讲解

碳酸铜(II)吸收热量后分解，形成氧化铜(II)和二氧化碳。按照本章定义，吸收热量属于吸热反应。

Penjelasan BM

Kuprum(II) karbonat menyerap haba lalu terurai membentuk kuprum(II) oksida dan karbon dioksida. Mengikut takrif bab ini, penyerapan haba menunjukkan tindak balas endotermik.
Copper(II) carbonate → copper(II) oxide + carbon dioxide
Kuprum(II) karbonat → kuprum(II) oksida + karbon dioksida

Answering steps

Read initial and final temperatures accurately.
Calculate or compare final − initial.
Positive change: heat released, exothermic. Negative change: heat absorbed, endothermic.
For variables, name the object together with its parameter: “type of chemicals”, “final temperature”, “volume of water”.
For hypothesis, connect manipulated variable and responding variable in one sentence.
