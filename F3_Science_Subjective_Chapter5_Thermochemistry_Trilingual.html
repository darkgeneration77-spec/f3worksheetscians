Practice 1 • Exothermic vs Endothermic Reactions

Exothermic reaction

(a) Exothermic reaction
(b) releases heat into the surroundings
(c) Temperature increases
(d) Container becomes hot
(e) Total energy content of products is less than reactants.
华语：放热反应，释放热量，温度上升，容器变热，生成物总能量较低。
BM: tindak balas eksotermik; membebaskan; meningkat; panas; kurang.

Endothermic reaction

(a) Endothermic reaction
(b) absorbs heat from the surroundings
(c) Temperature decreases
(d) Container becomes cold
(e) Total energy content of products is higher than reactants.
华语：吸热反应，吸收热量，温度下降，容器变冷，生成物总能量较高。
BM: tindak balas endotermik; menyerap; menurun; sejuk; lebih.
