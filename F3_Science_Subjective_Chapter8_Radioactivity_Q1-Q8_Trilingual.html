Question 1 — Discovery, Radioactivity and Half-life

Circle the scientist involved in the findings: i. First person who discovered X-ray. ii. Discovery of polonium and radium. iii. Physicist who discovered radioactivity.
i. Wilhelm Conrad Roentgen ii. Marie Curie iii. Antoine Henri Becquerel
EnglishRoentgen is linked to X-rays. Marie Curie is linked to polonium and radium. Antoine Henri Becquerel is linked to the discovery of radioactivity.
华语Roentgen 对应 X 射线；Marie Curie 对应钋和镭；Antoine Henri Becquerel 对应放射性的发现。先抓“发现了什么”，再配科学家。
Bahasa MelayuRoentgen dikaitkan dengan sinar-X. Marie Curie dengan polonium dan radium. Antoine Henri Becquerel dengan penemuan keradioaktifan.

1(b) Correct / Incorrect

Mark (✓) for the correct statement and (X) for the incorrect statement about radioactivity.
i. X ii. ✓ iii. ✓ iv. X
EnglishAlpha ray is a radioactive ray, so statement (i) is incorrect. Radioactivity is a spontaneous decay process of an unstable nucleus, so (ii) is correct. Becquerel (Bq) is the S.I. unit, so (iii) is correct. The uploaded answer marks (iv) incorrect.
华语α 射线属于放射性辐射，所以(i)错。放射性是不稳定原子核自发衰变，所以(ii)对。Becquerel（Bq）是SI单位，所以(iii)对。上传答案把(iv)判为错。

1(c) Activity and half-life

The graph shows activity against time for radioactive substance Q. i. What is the initial activity? ii. What is the activity at one half-life?
Initial activity = 100 Bq Activity at half-life = 100/2 = 50 Bq
EnglishRead the graph at time zero to get 100 Bq. One half-life means the activity becomes half its original value: 100 ÷ 2 = 50 Bq.
华语时间0时的初始活度是100 Bq。一个半衰期后变成原值一半：100 ÷ 2 = 50 Bq。
Bahasa MelayuPada masa sifar, aktiviti ialah 100 Bq. Selepas satu separuh hayat, aktiviti menjadi separuh: 100 ÷ 2 = 50 Bq.
口诀: Half-life = 原值 → 一半 → 再一半。

Question 2 — Atom and Ion

2(a) Draw an atom

Draw an atom with 4 protons, 4 electrons and 5 neutrons.
Nucleus: 4 protons + 5 neutrons Outside nucleus: 4 electrons
EnglishPut protons and neutrons inside the nucleus and electrons outside. Equal numbers of protons and electrons mean the atom is neutral.
华语原子核内画4个质子和5个中子；核外画4个电子。质子数=电子数，所以原子中性。
Bahasa MelayuLetakkan 4 proton dan 5 neutron dalam nukleus, serta 4 elektron di luar nukleus. Proton sama dengan elektron, jadi atom neutral.

2(b) Positive and negative ions

Positive ion: donates electrons Negative ion: receives electrons
EnglishLosing electrons leaves more positive charge, producing a positive ion. Gaining electrons adds negative charge, producing a negative ion.
华语失去电子 → 正电相对更多 → 正离子；得到电子 → 负电增加 → 负离子。
Bahasa MelayuKehilangan elektron menghasilkan ion positif. Menerima elektron menghasilkan ion negatif.
口诀: 失电子正，得电子负。

2(c) L, M, N and O

L: 4p, 4e M: 12p, 12e N: 8p, 10e O: 29p, 27e State two neutral atoms, identify the negatively charged particle and explain how O can become neutral.
Neutral atoms: L and M Negatively charged particle: N, because N has more electrons than protons. O becomes neutral by receiving 2 electrons.
EnglishNeutral means protons = electrons. N has two extra electrons, so it is negative. O needs two more electrons so that 29 electrons equal 29 protons.
华语中性条件是质子数=电子数。N多2个电子，所以带负电。O再得到2个电子，就会变成29电子=29质子。
Bahasa MelayuNeutral bermaksud proton = elektron. N mempunyai dua elektron berlebihan, jadi negatif. O perlu menerima 2 elektron supaya 29 elektron = 29 proton.

Question 3 — Ionising Radiation

3(a) Non-ionising radiation

Microwave Radio wave Infrared
EnglishThese are non-ionising radiation examples from the uploaded answer. They normally do not have enough energy to remove electrons from atoms.
华语上传答案给出的非电离辐射：微波、无线电波、红外线。它们通常没有足够能量把电子从原子移走。
Bahasa MelayuContoh sinaran tidak mengion ialah gelombang mikro, gelombang radio dan inframerah. Tenaganya biasanya tidak mencukupi untuk menyingkir elektron.

3(b) Alpha, beta and gamma

Alpha: Positive | Ionising High | Penetrating Low Beta: Negative | Ionising Medium | Penetrating Medium Gamma: Neutral | Ionising Low | Penetrating High
EnglishIonising power: α > β > γ. Penetrating power: γ > β > α. Charge: α positive, β negative, γ neutral.
华语电离能力：α > β > γ；穿透能力：γ > β > α；电荷：α正、β负、γ中性。
Bahasa MelayuKuasa pengionan: α > β > γ. Kuasa penembusan: γ > β > α. Cas: alfa positif, beta negatif, gama neutral.
口诀: 电离顺序 αβγ；穿透反过来 γβα。

3(c) Deflection in electric field

P = Beta ray Q = Gamma ray R = Alpha ray
EnglishBeta is negative, so it bends toward the positive plate. Gamma is neutral and travels straight. Alpha is positive and bends toward the negative plate.
华语β带负电 → 偏向正极板；γ中性 → 直线；α带正电 → 偏向负极板。
Bahasa MelayuBeta negatif → terpesong ke plat positif. Gama neutral → lurus. Alfa positif → terpesong ke plat negatif.

Question 6 — Radioactive Decay, Uses and Safety

6(a) Meaning of radioactive decay

Radioactive decay is a spontaneous process where an unstable nucleus emits radioactive rays until the nucleus becomes more stable.
EnglishMarking keywords: spontaneous process, unstable nucleus, emits radioactive rays, becomes more stable.
华语得分关键词：自发过程、不稳定原子核、放出放射性辐射、变得更稳定。
Bahasa MelayuKata kunci: proses spontan, nukleus tidak stabil, memancarkan sinaran radioaktif, menjadi lebih stabil.

EnglishCurie (Ci) is the earlier unit given in the answer. Becquerel (Bq) is the S.I. unit. Half-life is the time for undecayed nuclei to reduce to half the original value.

Question 8 — Half-life Calculations

8(a) Thorium-234

Half-life of Thorium-234 = 24 days. Original mass = 20 g. Calculate mass after second half-life.
0 day → 20 g 24 days → 10 g 48 days → 5 g Answer: 5 g
EnglishEach half-life halves the remaining mass: 20 → 10 → 5 g. Two half-lives = 48 days.
华语每经过一个半衰期，剩余质量减半：20→10→5 g。两个半衰期=48天。
Bahasa MelayuSetiap separuh hayat membahagi baki jisim kepada dua: 20→10→5 g. Dua separuh hayat = 48 hari.

8(b) Gallium-65

After 1 hour, Gallium-65 has decayed 75% of its original mass. Find its half-life.
75% decayed → 25% remains 25% remaining = two half-lives 2T½ = 1 hour T½ = 30 minutes
EnglishAfter one half-life, 50% remains. After two half-lives, 25% remains. Therefore 1 hour represents two half-lives, so one half-life is 30 minutes.
华语一个半衰期剩50%；两个半衰期剩25%。所以1小时=两个半衰期，一个半衰期=30分钟。
Bahasa MelayuSelepas satu separuh hayat baki 50%; selepas dua separuh hayat baki 25%. Maka 1 jam = 2 separuh hayat, jadi satu separuh hayat = 30 minit.
口诀: 75% decayed = 25% remains = 2 half-lives.
