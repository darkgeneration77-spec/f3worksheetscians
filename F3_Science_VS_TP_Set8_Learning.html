Section B — Answer Review

1. Reflection of light

Relationship: The angle of reflection is directly proportional to the angle of incidence.
For 70° incidence, angle of reflection = 70°.
Wooden block: light is absorbed by the wooden block.
Application: road signboards.

2. Biological control

Biological control: a method of controlling pest organisms by using the natural predator of the pest.
Disadvantage: may disrupt the natural food chain.
Interaction: prey-predator.

Section C — Structured Answer Review

1. Menstrual cycle

Menstruation days: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
Cycle S is the menstrual cycle, starts from puberty, occurs every 28 days, and involves changes to the lining of the uterine wall.
The cycle prepares the uterus for implantation of an embryo and allows breakdown/discharge of the uterine lining and blood if fertilisation does not occur.
If ovulation has not occurred, a mature egg/ovum is not released into the fallopian tube.

2. Digestion and absorption

Hydrochloric acid in gastric juice acts as a first line of defence by eliminating pathogens in food.
Organ Q = small intestine, which provides a large total surface area for nutrient absorption.
It has lacteals to absorb fatty acids, glycerol and fat-soluble vitamins.
Digested food absorbed is taken via the hepatic portal vein to the liver for assimilation.

3. Pressure

The supplied answer gives the calculated pressures for the first two situations as 2.5 × 10⁷ N m⁻² and 2.5 × 10⁵ N m⁻².
Conclusion: pressure increases when surface area decreases.
If force increases, pressure also increases because pressure is directly proportional to applied force.
For the 10 kg cube of side 6 cm: 2.7 × 10⁴ N m⁻².

4. Cathode ray oscilloscope

Functions: measure short time intervals and display waveforms.
X = Y-gain, used to set the magnitude of the vertical shift of the spot on the screen.
The answer file shows a sinusoidal waveform when connected to a house socket.
Homes use alternating current. Its voltage can be stepped up/down by transformers; power-station voltage is stepped up to lower current and reduce heat-energy loss in cables over long distances.

5. Refraction of light

Phenomenon: Refraction.
Glass is denser than air. When light travels from air into glass, its speed decreases and it bends towards the normal. When it travels from glass into air, its speed increases and it bends away from the normal.

6. Phloem tissue

Tissue X = phloem tissue.
Sieve tube cells are supported by companion cells, which provide nutrients and energy to the sieve tube cells.
After a ring of bark is removed, the upper part of the stem bulges because phloem has been removed and photosynthesis products cannot be transported away, so they accumulate.
Process: Translocation.
