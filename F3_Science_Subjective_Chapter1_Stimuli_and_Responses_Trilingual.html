Practice 1 — Human Ear

(i) Identify Q and R

The diagram shows the structure of the human ear. Identify the parts labelled Q and R.
Q: Ossicles / Osikel R: Cochlea / Koklea
EnglishQ points to the tiny bones of the middle ear, the ossicles. R points to the spiral-shaped cochlea in the inner ear.
华语Q 是中耳里的听小骨（ossicles）；R 是内耳蜗牛形状的耳蜗（cochlea）。看图时先认“细小骨头”和“蜗牛形”。
Bahasa MelayuQ ialah osikel di telinga tengah manakala R ialah koklea yang berbentuk seperti siput di telinga dalam.
ossiclescochleamiddle earinner ear

(ii) Function of P

State the function of the part labelled P.
Collects and sends sound waves into the ear canal. Mengumpul dan menghantar gelombang bunyi ke dalam salur telinga.
EnglishP is the outer ear structure that collects incoming sound waves and directs them into the ear canal.
华语P 的作用是收集外界声波，再把声波引导进入耳道。作答时要同时写出“collects”和“sends into ear canal”。
Bahasa MelayuP mengumpul gelombang bunyi dan menghantarnya ke dalam salur telinga.

(iii) Dizziness after taking a lift

Kee Seng feels dizzy after taking the lift in a hotel. What part of the ear is disturbed? Give an explanation.
Semicircular canals. This part of the ear controls the body balance. Salur separuh bulat. Bahagian telinga ini mengawal keseimbangan badan.
EnglishThe uploaded answer links dizziness with the semicircular canals because they control body balance.
华语头晕对应半规管（semicircular canals）。关键理由只有一个：它负责身体平衡。
Bahasa MelayuPening dikaitkan dengan salur separuh bulat kerana bahagian ini mengawal keseimbangan badan.
记忆： dizzy → balance → semicircular canals.

(iv) Hearing loss in one ear

An old man loses his hearing in one ear. What problem will he encounter during conversation with his family members?
The old man cannot determine the direction of sound accurately. Orang tua itu tidak dapat menentukan arah bunyi dengan tepat.
EnglishWith hearing lost in one ear, the answer key states that he cannot determine the direction of sound accurately.
华语单耳听力丧失时，主要问题是不能准确判断声音来自哪个方向。
Bahasa MelayuKehilangan pendengaran pada sebelah telinga menyebabkan arah bunyi sukar ditentukan dengan tepat.

Practice 2 — Human Eye and Sight

(a) Path of the sight mechanism

Complete the path from the cornea to the retina: Cornea → (i) → Pupil → (ii) → (iii) → Retina
(i) Aqueous humour / Gelemir (ii) Eye lens / Kanta mata (iii) Vitreous humour / Gelemaca
EnglishFollow the structures in order from the front of the eye toward the retina: cornea → aqueous humour → pupil → eye lens → vitreous humour → retina.
华语从眼球前方向后记顺序：角膜 → 房水 → 瞳孔 → 晶状体 → 玻璃体 → 视网膜。
Bahasa MelayuUrutan laluan ialah kornea → gelemir → anak mata → kanta mata → gelemaca → retina.

(b) Protecting eyes and sight

Suggest how to protect your eyes and sight.
Do not read in dim light and keep a distance between the eyes and the objects to read. Jangan baca dalam keadaan malap dan tetapkan satu jarak di antara mata dengan bahan bacaan.
EnglishThe supplied answer gives two linked precautions: avoid reading in dim light and keep a suitable distance from reading material.
华语答案要点：不要在昏暗环境阅读，并保持眼睛与阅读材料之间适当距离。
Bahasa MelayuElakkan membaca dalam cahaya malap dan kekalkan jarak yang sesuai antara mata dengan bahan bacaan.

(c) Long-term risk from phone screen and earphones

Amin frequently watches videos on his mobile phone screen and uses earphones. What risk may he face over a long period of time?
His senses of sight and hearing may be adversely affected. Deria penglihatan dan pendengarannya mungkin terjejas.
EnglishThe answer key combines both effects: prolonged screen viewing may affect sight, while prolonged earphone use may affect hearing.
华语题目同时给出手机屏幕和耳机，所以答案也要覆盖两个感官：视力和听力都可能受影响。
Bahasa MelayuPenggunaan skrin dan fon telinga secara kerap dalam tempoh lama boleh menjejaskan penglihatan serta pendengaran.

Practice 3 — Stereoscopic and Monocular Vision

Complete the comparison between stereoscopic vision and monocular vision.
AspectStereoscopic visionMonocular vision
Position of eyesIn front of the headSide of the head
Type of image formedThree-dimensional imageTwo-dimensional image
Estimate distance accuratelyCanCannot
Type of animalPredatorPrey
Types of organismsHumans, predatorsMost prey animals
ImportanceHelps predators to hunt preyHelps prey to escape from predators
EnglishThe uploaded answer contrasts front-facing eyes and 3D vision in predators with side-positioned eyes and 2D vision in prey. Stereoscopic vision supports accurate distance estimation for hunting, while monocular vision helps prey monitor a wider area and escape.
华语立体视觉：眼睛在前方、形成三维图像、可以准确估计距离，常见于人类和捕食者，有利于捕猎。单眼视觉：眼睛在头部两侧、形成二维图像、不能准确估计距离，常见于猎物，有利于逃避捕食者。
Bahasa MelayuPenglihatan stereoskopik mempunyai mata di hadapan kepala, imej tiga dimensi dan boleh menganggar jarak dengan tepat. Penglihatan monokular mempunyai mata di sisi kepala, imej dua dimensi dan tidak dapat menganggar jarak dengan tepat.
记忆： Predator = front + 3D + can estimate distance; Prey = side + 2D + cannot estimate accurately.

Question 4 — Stimulus, Receptor and Effector

(a) Organs involved

A huge dog barks towards Najihah. Mark the organs involved in the response towards the stimuli.
Eye / Mata Ear / Telinga
EnglishThe supplied key selects the eye and ear because the situation contains visual and sound stimuli.
华语大狗既能被“看见”，也能被“听见”，所以参与接收刺激的感觉器官是眼睛和耳朵。
Bahasa MelayuMata dan telinga terlibat kerana situasi tersebut mempunyai rangsangan penglihatan dan bunyi.

(b) Impulse pathway

Stimulus → (i) → Brain → (ii) → Responses
(i) Affector / Afektor (ii) Effector / Efektor
EnglishUse the exact uploaded-answer terms for this worksheet: affector receives the stimulus before the brain, and effector carries out the response after processing.
华语按照这份上传答案的用词：刺激先到 affector（Afektor），大脑处理后由 effector（Efektor）产生反应。
Bahasa MelayuIkut istilah dalam skema yang dimuat naik: rangsangan → afektor → otak → efektor → gerak balas.

Question 5 — Tropism

(a) Types of tropism

State the type of tropism response for each stimulus: Light, Gravity, Water.
Light: Phototropism / Fototropisme Gravity: Geotropism / Geotropisme Water: Hydrotropism / Hidrotropisme
EnglishMatch the stimulus directly to its tropism name: light → phototropism, gravity → geotropism, water → hydrotropism.
华语直接配对：光 → 向光性；重力 → 向地性；水 → 向水性。
Bahasa MelayuCahaya → fototropisme; graviti → geotropisme; air → hidrotropisme.

(b) Positive and negative geotropism

What is meant by positive geotropism and negative geotropism?
Positive geotropism is the growth of parts of plant towards gravity. Geotropisme positif ialah pertumbuhan bahagian tumbuhan ke arah graviti. Negative geotropism is the growth of parts of plant against the direction of gravity. Geotropisme negatif ialah pertumbuhan bahagian tumbuhan menentang arah graviti.
EnglishPositive means growth toward the stimulus direction. Negative means growth against the stimulus direction.
华语“正”表示朝向重力方向生长；“负”表示逆着重力方向生长。
Bahasa MelayuPositif bermaksud tumbuh ke arah graviti, manakala negatif bermaksud tumbuh menentang arah graviti.
