Section B — Answer + Reasoning

B1. Organs involved in homeostasis
Supplied answers: Lungs, Kidney, Skin, Pancreas.
Why?
• Lungs help regulate carbon dioxide concentration in the blood.
• Kidneys regulate water and salt balance and remove urea.
• Skin regulates body temperature through sweating and changes in blood flow.
• Pancreas regulates blood glucose through insulin and glucagon.
华语：稳态就是把身体内部条件维持在适合范围。肺管 CO₂，肾管水盐与尿素，皮肤管体温，胰脏管血糖。
Keyword: homeostasis = maintaining a stable internal environment
B2. Batteries and bulb brightness
Answers: connected in series; more batteries → brighter bulb; graph later drops; six batteries in parallel → No.
How to read it: In series, battery voltages add. Higher total potential difference can drive a larger current through the bulb, so it becomes brighter. If too many batteries are used, the bulb may fail, which explains the sudden drop in the graph. In parallel, identical 2 V batteries still provide about 2 V across the bulb rather than adding to 12 V, so the supplied answer is No.
华语口诀：串联“电压相加”；并联“电压不叠加”。所以串联越多通常越亮，太高可能烧灯泡。
Keyword: series → voltage adds
B3. Sea breeze and convection
Answers: Area Y; sea water changes temperature less than land; sea breeze; convection.
Reasoning: During daytime, land heats faster than the sea. Air above land becomes warmer, expands, becomes less dense and rises. Cooler, denser air above the sea moves toward land to replace it. That moving air is a sea breeze. The circulation of warm and cool air is convection.
华语：白天陆地升温快，陆地上方热空气上升；海面上方较冷空气吹向陆地，所以是海风。空气整体循环就是对流。
口诀：白天 Land hot → air rises → Sea air comes in
B4. Spring
Stored energy: Elastic potential energy.
When a spring is stretched or compressed, work is done on it and energy is stored due to its deformation. A stiffer spring resists deformation more strongly. The supplied acceptable ways include a smaller coil diameter, larger wire diameter and shorter spring.
华语：弹簧被拉长或压缩时会储存“弹性势能”。越难拉伸，代表弹簧越硬。
Keyword: deformation → elastic potential energy
B5. Satellite information sequence
Answer: 2 → 1 → 4 → 3
Step 2 must come first because the transmitter station sends the signal to the satellite. Then step 1: the satellite receives, amplifies and changes the frequency. Next step 4: the signal is sent back to Earth. Finally step 3: the receiving station receives it.
华语：发射站 → 卫星处理 → 卫星回传地球 → 接收站。所以是 2-1-4-3。
口诀：Send up → process → send down → receive

C1. Reproductive system
1. B = Prostate gland. It secretes a milky white fluid that activates or increases sperm mobility.
The label B is below the urinary bladder around the upper urethra, which matches the position of the prostate gland. Its secretion forms part of semen and helps sperm function and movement.
华语：看位置，膀胱下方包围尿道的位置就是前列腺；它的分泌液帮助精子活动。

2. Sperm vs ovum.
Similarity: both are reproductive cells and their production involves cell division.
Difference: sperm is produced in testes, ovum in ovaries; sperm has a tail, ovum has no tail and is round.
To compare cells, use two axes: where produced and structure. Sperm needs a tail for movement toward the ovum; the ovum is a large, non-motile reproductive cell.

3. Female puberty changes: acne; development of breasts and hips.
Exam pattern: name + function / similarity + difference / two physical changes
C2. White blood cells and immunity
Cell: White blood cells.
Ways to kill pathogens: produce antibodies; ingest and digest pathogens using enzymes.
The diagram shows several types of white blood cells. One defence method is antibody production. Another is phagocytosis, where the cell engulfs the pathogen and digests it with enzymes.
华语：白血球主要两招：制造抗体；吞噬病原体后用酶消化。
Immunity: the ability of the body system to fight against pathogens before the body is infected by them.
Types: naturally acquired active, artificially acquired active, naturally acquired passive, artificially acquired passive immunity.
Classify immunity using two questions: Who makes the antibody? If your body makes it, it is active. If antibody is received from another source, it is passive. Then ask whether it happens naturally or artificially.
口诀：自己做抗体 = active；拿别人抗体 = passive
C3. Heating and cooling 150 ml of water
1. Time to boil = 10 minutes.
From the graph, heating starts around 6 min at A and reaches 100°C at B around 16 min. So 16 − 6 = 10 min.
2. BC: liquid → gas.
BC is the flat section at 100°C. Temperature stays constant while energy is used to overcome intermolecular attraction and change water into steam.
3. Volume at CD is less than AB.
Some water has already changed into steam during BC and escaped, so less liquid water remains afterward.
4. Using 300 ml takes longer.
There are more water molecules, so more heat energy is needed to raise the temperature and complete the same heating/cooling process.
5. Add salt: maximum temperature slightly above 100°C and minimum slightly below 0°C.
The supplied answer reflects boiling-point elevation and freezing-point depression when salt is dissolved in water.
华语：加盐后，沸点升高、冰点降低。
Graph skill: flat line during change of state ≠ no energy transfer
C4. Eye and vision
1. Ciliary muscle function: changes the thickness of the eye lens.
Ciliary muscles control lens shape so the eye can focus light from objects at different distances onto the retina.
2. Reading a newspaper: ciliary muscles contract; lens becomes more curved and thicker.
A newspaper is a near object. A thicker, more curved lens has greater refracting power, which is needed to focus near light rays on the retina.
3. Eyeball too long: short-sightedness.
If the eyeball is too long, light from a distant object is focused before reaching the retina, so distant objects appear blurred.
4. Correction: concave lens.
A concave lens diverges incoming light slightly before it enters the eye, moving the focus backward onto the retina.
Source note: One later line in the supplied answer file describes “cannot see near objects clearly but can see far objects clearly”, which describes hypermetropia, while the earlier Set 7 question about John says he sees near objects clearly but far objects are blurred. The page now keeps the supplied answers but separates these two conditions so students are not forced to memorise a contradiction.
Short-sighted: far blur → concave lens. Long-sighted: near blur → convex lens.
C5. Cellular respiration and photosynthesis
Cell respiration: cells use oxygen and glucose to produce energy, carbon dioxide and water.
Oxygen + Glucose → Energy + Carbon dioxide + Water
Glucose stores chemical energy. In cellular respiration, glucose is oxidised and its stored energy is released for cell activities.
Respiration vs breathing: Not the same.
Breathing is a mechanical process that brings oxygen into the respiratory system and removes carbon dioxide. Cellular respiration is a chemical process in cells that uses oxygen to release energy from glucose.
Photosynthesis comparison:
Similarity: both involve gas exchange; supplied answer also states both require energy.
Differences: photosynthesis depends on sunlight, uses water + carbon dioxide, produces glucose + oxygen, and occurs in chloroplasts; cellular respiration uses glucose + oxygen, produces water + carbon dioxide + energy, and occurs in mitochondria.
华语：呼吸动作只是“吸进/呼出”；细胞呼吸是真正把葡萄糖里的能量释放出来。
Keyword pair: chloroplast = photosynthesis; mitochondria = cellular respiration
C6. Iodine-131 and beta radiation
1. Decay equation: unstable iodine atom undergoes radioactive decay to become a more stable xenon atom by emitting beta particles.
A beta particle is a fast-moving electron. During beta decay, the nucleus changes and the atom becomes a different element.
2. Half-life: time taken for the number of undecayed nuclei to reduce to half its original number.
Half-life always means “halve the undecayed nuclei”, not “all nuclei disappear”.
3. Original number of nuclei:
24 ÷ 8 = 3 half-lives
1.25 × 2³ = 10 million
We are working backward in time. Every half-life backward means double the number: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million.
4. Penetration: beta passes through paper but not aluminium and lead.
The supplied answer classifies beta as having moderate penetrating power: stronger than alpha, weaker than gamma.
5. Charge: negative.
Beta particles are fast-moving electrons, and electrons carry negative charge.
口诀：α 最弱穿透；β 中等；γ 最强。β = electron = negative
