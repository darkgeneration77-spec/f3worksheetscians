Section B — Answer + Why

B1 Homeostasis organs

Lungs / Peparu; Kidney / Ginjal; Skin / Kulit; Pancreas / Pankreas
English: These organs regulate internal conditions: lungs regulate CO₂, kidneys water/salts, skin body temperature and pancreas blood glucose.
华语：这些器官帮助维持稳态：肺调节 CO₂，肾调节水和盐，皮肤调节体温，胰脏调节血糖。
BM: Organ ini mengekalkan homeostasis: peparu mengawal CO₂, ginjal air dan garam, kulit suhu badan, pankreas glukosa darah.

B2 Batteries

Series; brightness increases; increases then decreases; No.
English: In series, voltages add. In parallel, identical 2 V batteries still provide about 2 V, not 12 V.
华语：串联电池电压相加；相同 2 V 电池并联后仍约 2 V，不会变成 12 V。
BM: Dalam sambungan bersiri voltan dijumlahkan. Bateri 2 V secara selari masih memberi kira-kira 2 V, bukan 12 V.

B3 Sea breeze

Area Y; lower change in sea-water temperature; Sea breeze; Convection.
English: Land heats faster. Warm air over land rises and cooler sea air moves toward land.
华语：白天陆地升温较快，热空气上升，海面较冷空气吹向陆地，形成海风，属于对流。
BM: Daratan memanas lebih cepat. Udara panas naik dan udara sejuk dari laut bergerak ke darat, menghasilkan bayu laut melalui perolakan.

B4 Spring

Elastic potential energy / 弹性势能 / Tenaga keupayaan kenyal
English: A stretched spring is deformed and stores elastic potential energy. Supplied accepted methods: smaller spring diameter, larger wire diameter and shorter spring.
华语：弹簧拉长后发生形变，因此储存弹性势能。原答案接受：弹簧直径较小、线径较大、弹簧较短。
BM: Spring yang diregang menyimpan tenaga keupayaan kenyal. Jawapan asal: diameter spring lebih kecil, diameter dawai lebih besar dan spring lebih pendek.

B5 Satellite

2 → 1 → 4 → 3
English: Transmitter sends signal → satellite receives/amplifies → signal sent back to Earth → receiving station receives it.
华语：地面发送 → 卫星接收并处理 → 卫星回传地球 → 接收站接收，所以是 2-1-4-3。
BM: Stesen pemancar menghantar → satelit menerima/menguatkan → isyarat dihantar semula ke Bumi → stesen penerima menerima.

Section C — Structured Questions

C1 Reproductive system

B = Prostate gland. Secretes milky white fluid that activates/increases sperm mobility.
English: Both sperm and ovum are reproductive cells. Sperm is produced by testes and has a tail; ovum is produced by ovaries, has no tail and is round.
华语：精子与卵细胞都是生殖细胞。精子由睾丸产生并有尾巴；卵细胞由卵巢产生，无尾且呈圆形。
BM: Sperma dan ovum ialah sel pembiakan. Sperma dihasilkan oleh testis dan berekor; ovum dihasilkan oleh ovari, tiada ekor dan bulat.

C2 Immunity

White blood cells; produce antibodies; ingest and digest pathogens with enzymes.
English: White blood cells protect the body by antibody production and phagocytosis.
华语：白血球通过制造抗体以及吞噬、消化病原体来保护身体。
BM: Sel darah putih melindungi badan melalui penghasilan antibodi dan fagositosis.

C3 Heating and cooling water

10 min; liquid → gas; CD less water than AB; 300 ml takes longer; salt water max >100°C and min <0°C.
English: Some water becomes steam during BC, so less remains at CD. More water needs more heat energy. Salt raises boiling point and lowers freezing point.
华语：BC 时部分水变成水蒸气，所以 CD 剩余水量较少。水越多，需要的热能越多；盐会提高沸点并降低冰点。
BM: Sebahagian air menjadi wap semasa BC. Lebih banyak air memerlukan lebih banyak tenaga haba. Garam menaikkan takat didih dan menurunkan takat beku.

C4 Eye and vision

Ciliary muscle changes lens thickness; near object → contracts, lens thicker; long eyeball → short-sightedness; concave lens.
English: A long eyeball focuses distant light in front of the retina, so a concave lens is used.
华语：眼球过长会使远处光线聚焦在视网膜前，造成近视，因此用凹透镜矫正。
BM: Bola mata terlalu panjang menyebabkan cahaya objek jauh difokuskan di hadapan retina, maka kanta cekung digunakan.

C5 Cellular respiration

Oxygen + Glucose → Energy + Carbon dioxide + Water
English: Cellular respiration oxidises glucose to release energy. Breathing is the mechanical intake of oxygen, so they are not the same process.
华语：细胞呼吸氧化葡萄糖并释放能量；呼吸动作只是机械性吸入氧气，所以两者不同。
BM: Respirasi sel mengoksidakan glukosa untuk membebaskan tenaga; pernafasan ialah proses mekanikal mengambil oksigen.

C6 Radioactivity

Iodine-131 → more stable xenon + beta particle.
English: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 half-lives. Work backwards: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei. Beta penetrates paper but not aluminium/lead and is negatively charged because it is a fast-moving electron.
华语：24 ÷ 8 = 3 个半衰期。反推：1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei。β 可穿过纸但不能穿过铝和铅，而且带负电，因为它是高速电子。
BM: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 separuh hayat. Kira songsang: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 juta nukleus. Beta menembusi kertas tetapi bukan aluminium/plumbum dan bercas negatif kerana merupakan elektron bergerak pantas.
