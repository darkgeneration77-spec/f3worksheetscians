A18 Work done

B — 100 J
English: Work = force × displacement = 50 N × 2 m = 100 J. The 1 kg mass is unnecessary because force is already given.
华语：功 = 力 × 位移 = 50 × 2 = 100 J。题目已经给 50 N，所以 1 kg 不需要使用。
BM: Kerja = daya × sesaran = 50 N × 2 m = 100 J. Jisim 1 kg tidak diperlukan kerana daya sudah diberi.

Section B

B1 Homeostasis

Lungs / Peparu; Kidney / Ginjal; Skin / Kulit; Pancreas / Pankreas
English: These organs regulate internal conditions: lungs regulate CO₂, kidneys water/salts, skin body temperature, pancreas blood glucose.
华语：这些器官帮助维持稳态：肺调节 CO₂，肾调节水和盐，皮肤调节体温，胰脏调节血糖。
BM: Organ ini mengekalkan homeostasis: peparu mengawal CO₂, ginjal air dan garam, kulit suhu badan, pankreas glukosa darah.

B2 Batteries

Series; brightness increases; increases then decreases; No.
English: In series, battery voltages add. In parallel, identical 2 V batteries still give about 2 V, not 12 V.
华语：串联电池电压相加；相同 2 V 电池并联后仍约 2 V，不会变成 12 V。
BM: Dalam sambungan bersiri voltan dijumlahkan. Bateri 2 V secara selari masih memberi kira-kira 2 V, bukan 12 V.

B3 Sea breeze

Area Y; lower change in sea-water temperature; Sea breeze; Convection.
English: Land heats faster. Warm air over land rises and cooler sea air moves toward land. This is sea breeze caused by convection.
华语：白天陆地升温较快，热空气上升，海面较冷空气吹向陆地，形成海风，属于对流。
BM: Daratan memanas lebih cepat. Udara panas naik dan udara sejuk dari laut bergerak ke darat, menghasilkan bayu laut melalui perolakan.

B4 Spring

Elastic potential energy / 弹性势能 / Tenaga keupayaan kenyal
English: A stretched spring is deformed and stores elastic potential energy. Original accepted methods for greater stiffness: smaller spring diameter, larger wire diameter, shorter spring.
华语：弹簧拉长后发生形变，因此储存弹性势能。原答案接受：弹簧直径较小、线径较大、弹簧较短。
BM: Spring yang diregang menyimpan tenaga keupayaan kenyal. Jawapan asal: diameter spring lebih kecil, diameter dawai lebih besar dan spring lebih pendek.

B5 Satellite

2 → 1 → 4 → 3

华语：按原图追踪讯号：地面发送 → 卫星接收 → 卫星回传 → 地面接收，所以是 2-1-4-3。

Section C

C1 Reproductive system

B = Prostate gland. Secretes milky white fluid that activates/increases sperm mobility.
English: Both sperm and ovum are reproductive cells and their production involves cell division. Sperm is produced by testes and has a tail; ovum is produced by ovaries, has no tail and is round. Puberty answers: acne; development of breasts and hips.
华语：精子与卵细胞都是生殖细胞，产生过程涉及细胞分裂。精子由睾丸产生并有尾巴；卵细胞由卵巢产生，无尾且呈圆形。青春期答案：长青春痘、乳房和臀部发育。
BM: Sperma dan ovum ialah sel pembiakan dan penghasilannya melibatkan pembahagian sel. Sperma dihasilkan oleh testis dan berekor; ovum dihasilkan oleh ovari, tiada ekor dan bulat. Akil baligh: jerawat serta perkembangan buah dada dan pinggul.

C2 Immunity

White blood cells; produce antibodies; ingest and digest pathogens with enzymes.
English: White blood cells defend against pathogens. The supplied types are naturally/artificially acquired active immunity and naturally/artificially acquired passive immunity.
华语：白血球制造抗体，并吞噬、消化病原体。原答案的免疫类型：天然主动、人工主动、天然被动、人工被动。
BM: Sel darah putih menghasilkan antibodi serta menelan dan mencerna patogen. Jenis keimunan: aktif semula jadi, aktif buatan, pasif semula jadi dan pasif buatan.

C3 Heating and cooling water

10 min; liquid → gas; CD less water than AB; 300 ml takes longer; salt water max >100°C and min <0°C.
English: Some water becomes steam during BC, so less remains at CD. More water needs more heat energy. Salt raises boiling point and lowers freezing point.
华语：BC 时部分水变成水蒸气，所以 CD 剩余水量较少。300 ml 水更多，需要更多热能。盐会提高沸点并降低冰点。
BM: Sebahagian air menjadi wap semasa BC, jadi isipadu CD lebih rendah. Lebih banyak air memerlukan lebih banyak tenaga haba. Garam menaikkan takat didih dan menurunkan takat beku.

C4 Eye and vision

Ciliary muscle changes lens thickness; near object → contracts, lens thicker; long eyeball → short-sightedness; concave lens.
English: For near vision the lens becomes thicker. A long eyeball causes distant rays to focus in front of the retina, producing short-sightedness. A concave lens corrects it.
华语：看近物时晶状体变厚。眼球过长会使远处光线聚焦在视网膜前，造成近视，用凹透镜矫正。
BM: Untuk objek dekat kanta menjadi lebih tebal. Bola mata terlalu panjang menyebabkan cahaya objek jauh difokuskan di hadapan retina, menghasilkan rabun jauh dan dibetulkan dengan kanta cekung.
Q5 supplied answer: cannot see near objects clearly but can see far objects clearly.

C5 Cellular respiration

Oxygen + Glucose → Energy + Carbon dioxide + Water
English: Cellular respiration oxidises glucose to release energy. Breathing is the mechanical intake of oxygen, so they are not the same process. Supplied comparison: photosynthesis uses water + CO₂ and produces glucose + O₂ in chloroplasts; respiration uses glucose + O₂ and produces water + CO₂ + energy in mitochondria.
华语：细胞呼吸氧化葡萄糖并释放能量；呼吸动作只是机械性吸入氧气，所以两者不同。光合作用在叶绿体中利用水和 CO₂ 制造葡萄糖和 O₂；细胞呼吸在线粒体中利用葡萄糖和 O₂，产生水、CO₂ 和能量。
BM: Respirasi sel mengoksidakan glukosa untuk membebaskan tenaga; pernafasan ialah proses mekanikal mengambil oksigen. Fotosintesis menggunakan air + CO₂ dan menghasilkan glukosa + O₂ dalam kloroplas; respirasi menggunakan glukosa + O₂ dan menghasilkan air + CO₂ + tenaga dalam mitokondria.

C6 Radioactivity

Iodine-131 → more stable xenon + beta particle.
English: Half-life = time for undecayed nuclei to reduce to half the original number. 24 ÷ 8 = 3 half-lives. Work backwards: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei. Beta penetrates paper but not aluminium/lead because penetrating power is moderate. Beta is negatively charged because it is a fast-moving high-energy electron.
华语：半衰期 = 未衰变原子核减少到原来一半所需时间。24 ÷ 8 = 3 个半衰期。反推：1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei。β 可穿过纸，但不能穿过铝和铅，因为穿透力中等。β 带负电，因为它是高速高能电子。
BM: Separuh hayat ialah masa untuk nukleus yang belum mereput berkurang kepada separuh bilangan asal. 24 ÷ 8 = 3 separuh hayat. Kira songsang: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 juta nukleus. Beta menembusi kertas tetapi bukan aluminium/plumbum kerana kuasa penembusan sederhana. Beta bercas negatif kerana merupakan elektron bertenaga tinggi yang bergerak pantas.
