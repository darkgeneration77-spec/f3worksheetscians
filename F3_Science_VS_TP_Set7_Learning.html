A18. Work done

Answer / 答案 / Jawapan: B — 100 J
English: Work done = Force × displacement = 50 N × 2 m = 100 J. The 1 kg mass is not needed because the pushing force is already given.
华语：功 = 力 × 位移。题目已经给 50 N 和 2 m，所以 50 × 2 = 100 J。1 kg 是干扰资料，不需要再算重量。
Bahasa Melayu: Kerja = Daya × sesaran = 50 N × 2 m = 100 J. Jisim 1 kg tidak perlu digunakan kerana daya tolakan telah diberi.
Keyword / 关键词 / Kata kunci: W = F × s

A19. Radioactive decay

Answer / 答案 / Jawapan: B (according to supplied key)
English: The question asks for the wrong statement. Radioactive decay is spontaneous and random and releases energetic radiation. Use the exact wording of the supplied answer key when marking this set.
华语：题目要求找错误陈述。做这类题先圈出 “wrong statement”，再逐项检查 radioactive decay 的定义与 radiation 的性质。

Section B — Answer + Why

B1. Homeostasis organs

Original answers: Lungs / peparu; Kidney / ginjal; Skin / kulit; Pancreas / pankreas.
English: These organs regulate internal conditions: lungs regulate CO₂, kidneys regulate water/salts and remove urea, skin regulates body temperature, pancreas regulates blood glucose.
华语：稳态就是维持身体内部环境稳定。肺调节 CO₂；肾调节水和盐并排出尿素；皮肤调节体温；胰脏调节血糖。
BM: Homeostasis mengekalkan persekitaran dalaman yang stabil. Peparu mengawal CO₂, ginjal mengawal air/garam dan urea, kulit mengawal suhu badan, pankreas mengawal glukosa darah.

B2. Batteries and bulb brightness

Original answers: connected in series; more batteries → brightness increases; later increases then decreases; six 2 V batteries in parallel → No.
English: In series, voltages add, so a larger potential difference can make the bulb brighter. In parallel, identical 2 V batteries still provide about 2 V, not 12 V.
华语：串联电池的电压会相加，所以灯泡通常更亮；并联相同 2 V 电池，电压仍约为 2 V，不会变成 12 V。
BM: Dalam sambungan bersiri, voltan dijumlahkan lalu mentol menjadi lebih terang. Dalam sambungan selari, bateri 2 V masih membekalkan kira-kira 2 V, bukan 12 V.
口诀 / Tip: Series = voltage adds / Bersiri = voltan bertambah

B3. Sea breeze

Original answers: Area Y; lower temperature change of sea water; Sea breeze; Convection.
English: Land heats faster during daytime. Warm air above land rises. Cooler, denser air above the sea moves toward land. This is sea breeze, caused by convection.
华语：白天陆地升温比海水快，陆地上方热空气上升，海面上方较冷空气吹向陆地，形成海风；空气循环属于对流。
BM: Pada waktu siang, daratan memanas lebih cepat. Udara panas di atas daratan naik dan udara sejuk dari laut bergerak ke darat. Fenomena ini ialah bayu laut dan berlaku melalui perolakan.

B4. Spring

Answer: Elastic potential energy / Tenaga keupayaan kenyal / 弹性势能.
Accepted methods in supplied key: smaller spring diameter; larger wire diameter; shorter spring.
English: Stretching or compressing a spring stores energy because the spring is deformed.
华语：弹簧被拉长或压缩时发生形变，因此储存弹性势能。
BM: Spring yang diregang atau dimampatkan mengalami perubahan bentuk lalu menyimpan tenaga keupayaan kenyal.

B5. Satellite information sequence

Answer: 2 → 1 → 4 → 3
English: Transmitter sends signal upward → satellite receives/processes it → satellite sends it back to Earth → receiving station receives it.
华语：发射站发送 → 卫星接收并处理 → 卫星回传地球 → 接收站接收，所以 2-1-4-3。
BM: Stesen pemancar menghantar isyarat → satelit menerima/memproses → satelit menghantar semula ke Bumi → stesen penerima menerima. Maka 2-1-4-3.

Section C — Structured Questions

C1. Reproductive system

Original answer: B = prostate gland. Secretes a milky white fluid that activates/increases sperm mobility. Similarities: both are reproductive cells; both production involves cell division. Differences: sperm from testes, ovum from ovaries; sperm has tail, ovum is round without tail. Puberty: acne; development of breasts and hips.
English: Identify the prostate by its position below the bladder. For comparison questions, organise marks into “similarity” and “difference”, then compare origin and structure.
华语：先看位置：膀胱下方的是前列腺。比较题不要乱写，先写共同点，再从“产生位置”和“结构”比较精子与卵细胞。
BM: Kenal pasti prostat melalui kedudukannya di bawah pundi kencing. Untuk soalan perbandingan, tulis persamaan dahulu kemudian beza dari segi tempat penghasilan dan struktur.

C2. White blood cells & immunity

Original answers: White blood cells; produce antibodies; ingest and digest pathogens with enzymes. Immunity = ability of body system to fight pathogens before infection. Types: naturally/artificially acquired active and naturally/artificially acquired passive immunity.
English: White blood cells defend the body mainly by antibody production and phagocytosis. Active immunity means your body produces antibodies; passive immunity means antibodies are received from another source.
华语：白血球主要靠制造抗体和吞噬病原体。主动免疫 = 自己制造抗体；被动免疫 = 从外界获得抗体。
BM: Sel darah putih menghasilkan antibodi dan menjalankan fagositosis. Keimunan aktif = badan menghasilkan antibodi sendiri; keimunan pasif = antibodi diterima daripada sumber lain.

C3. Heating and cooling water

Original answers: 10 minutes; liquid → gas; CD has less water than AB because some became steam during BC; 300 ml takes longer; salt water maximum slightly above 100°C and minimum slightly below 0°C.
English: Read time directly from the graph. A flat section at boiling means temperature stays constant while liquid changes to gas. More water needs more heat energy. Dissolved salt raises boiling point and lowers freezing point.
华语：先读图。沸腾时平台线表示温度不变，但水正在液态变气态。水越多，需要的热能越多；加盐会使沸点升高、冰点降低。
BM: Baca masa daripada graf. Bahagian mendatar semasa mendidih menunjukkan suhu kekal tetapi cecair berubah kepada gas. Lebih banyak air memerlukan lebih banyak tenaga haba. Garam menaikkan takat didih dan menurunkan takat beku.

C4. Eye and vision

Original answers: ciliary muscle changes lens thickness; near object → ciliary muscle contracts and lens becomes more curved/thicker; eyeball too long → short-sightedness; correction → concave lens.
English: A long eyeball focuses distant light in front of the retina. A concave lens diverges incoming rays so the final image can focus on the retina.
华语：眼球过长时，远处光线会在视网膜前聚焦，造成近视。凹透镜先让光线发散，使焦点移回视网膜。
BM: Bola mata terlalu panjang menyebabkan cahaya objek jauh difokuskan di hadapan retina. Kanta cekung mencapahkan cahaya supaya imej difokuskan pada retina.
Source note: The supplied answer file later contains a line saying “cannot see near objects clearly but can see far objects clearly”. That wording conflicts with the earlier John question, so it is not merged into the short-sightedness explanation.

C5. Cellular respiration

Original definition: cells use oxygen and glucose to produce energy, carbon dioxide and water.
Oxygen + Glucose → Energy + Carbon dioxide + Water
English: Breathing is the mechanical intake/exhalation of gases. Cellular respiration is a chemical process in cells that oxidises glucose and releases energy.
华语：呼吸动作是吸入和呼出气体；细胞呼吸是在细胞里氧化葡萄糖并释放能量，两者不是同一个过程。
BM: Pernafasan ialah proses mekanikal mengambil dan menghembus gas. Respirasi sel ialah proses kimia dalam sel yang mengoksidakan glukosa untuk membebaskan tenaga.
Photosynthesis vs respiration: photosynthesis depends on light, uses water + CO₂ and produces glucose + O₂ in chloroplasts; cellular respiration uses glucose + O₂ and produces water + CO₂ + energy in mitochondria.

C6. Iodine-131 & beta radiation

Original answers: unstable iodine decays into more stable xenon by emitting beta particles. Half-life = time for undecayed nuclei to reduce to half. Beta penetrates paper but not aluminium/lead. Beta is negatively charged and is a fast-moving high-energy electron.
Calculation: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 half-lives. Work backwards from 1.25 million: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei.
华语：24 ÷ 8 = 3 个半衰期。题目给的是现在剩下 1.25 million，要反推过去，所以每次 ×2：1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million。
BM: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 separuh hayat. Oleh sebab soalan meminta bilangan asal, kira secara songsang dengan ×2: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 juta nukleus.
口诀 / Tip: future → ÷2; backwards / songsang / 反推 → ×2
