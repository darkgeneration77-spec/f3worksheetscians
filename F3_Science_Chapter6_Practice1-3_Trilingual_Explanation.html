2. Energy changes

Nuclear power station: Nuclear energy → Heat energy → Kinetic energy → Electrical energy Hydroelectric power station: Gravitational potential energy → Kinetic energy → Electrical energy

English

In a nuclear power station, nuclear energy heats water. Steam gains kinetic energy and turns the turbine, while the generator converts motion into electrical energy. In a hydroelectric station, stored water has gravitational potential energy; falling water gains kinetic energy and turns the turbine.

华语

核电站中，核能先转化为热能，蒸汽获得动能并带动涡轮，发电机再把运动转化为电能。水力发电中，高处的水具有重力势能，下落后转为动能并推动涡轮。

Bahasa Melayu

Dalam stesen jana kuasa nuklear, tenaga nuklear memanaskan air. Wap memperoleh tenaga kinetik lalu memutarkan turbin, manakala penjana menukarkan gerakan kepada tenaga elektrik. Dalam stesen hidroelektrik, air tersimpan mempunyai tenaga keupayaan graviti dan menjadi tenaga kinetik apabila mengalir turun.
口诀：
Nuclear: N-H-K-E = Nuclear → Heat → Kinetic → Electrical.
Hydro: G-K-E = Gravitational → Kinetic → Electrical.
核：核→热→动→电；水：重力势→动→电。
kinetic energygravitational potential energyheat energyelectrical energyinduced currentalternating current

Answering steps

Start with the original energy source.
Follow the physical process: heating, motion, turbine rotation and generator output.
End with electrical energy.
For True/False, correct the false scientific term, not just write “False”.

Practice 3Transformer

(a) Symbol and labels

Primary coil = input side / 初级线圈 = 输入端 / gegelung primer = bahagian input
Secondary coil = output side / 次级线圈 = 输出端 / gegelung sekunder = bahagian output

(b) Name of transformer

Step-up transformer
升压变压器
Transformer injak naik

(c) Characteristics

VoltageInput voltage is lower than output voltage.
输入电压低于输出电压。
Voltan input lebih rendah daripada voltan output.
Number of turnsThe primary coil has fewer turns than the secondary coil.
初级线圈匝数少于次级线圈。
Bilangan lilitan pada gegelung primer kurang daripada gegelung sekunder.
Step-up transformer: Vₛ > Vₚ Nₛ > Nₚ Output voltage higher; secondary coil has more turns.

English

A step-up transformer increases voltage. Therefore, the secondary coil must have more turns than the primary coil. The input is connected to the primary coil and the output is obtained from the secondary coil.

华语

升压变压器提高电压，所以次级线圈的匝数必须多于初级线圈。输入接初级线圈，输出从次级线圈取得。

Bahasa Melayu

Transformer injak naik menaikkan voltan. Oleh itu, gegelung sekunder mesti mempunyai lebih banyak lilitan berbanding gegelung primer. Input disambungkan pada gegelung primer dan output diperoleh daripada gegelung sekunder.
口诀 / Mnemonic:
Step UP = voltage UP = secondary turns UP.
升压：输出电压高，次级匝数多。
Injak naik: voltan output tinggi, lilitan sekunder lebih banyak.
primary coilsecondary coilinput voltageoutput voltagestep-up transformernumber of turns

Answering steps

Identify input and output sides first.
Compare the number of turns in both coils.
More turns at secondary means step-up; fewer turns at secondary means step-down.
State the voltage comparison using “lower than” or “higher than”.
