Energy is the ability to do work.

Practice 2Potential and Kinetic Energy

(a) Swing energy changes

A = Potential energy
B = Kinetic energy
C = Potential energy

English

At the highest position, the swing has maximum potential energy. While moving downward, potential energy changes into kinetic energy. At the opposite high point, kinetic energy changes back into potential energy.

华语

秋千在最高点具有最大的势能；向下运动时，势能转化为动能；到另一侧最高点时，动能再转化为势能。

Bahasa Melayu

Pada kedudukan tertinggi, buaian mempunyai tenaga keupayaan maksimum. Semasa bergerak ke bawah, tenaga keupayaan bertukar kepada tenaga kinetik. Pada titik tinggi yang bertentangan, tenaga kinetik bertukar semula kepada tenaga keupayaan.

(b) Is kinetic energy involved?

Yes. All the activities involve movement.
是。所有活动都涉及运动。
Ya. Semua aktiviti melibatkan pergerakan.

(b) Worker A and Worker B

i. Force acting on the box: Gravitational force
ii. Energy possessed by the box: Gravitational potential energy
iii. Both workers do the same amount of work because the weight and height are the same.
iv. Worker A has more power because he takes a shorter time.

English

The work done is the same because both workers lift the same box through the same height. Power differs because power depends on time. Worker A takes 10 s, while worker B takes 15 s.

华语

两人搬的是同一个箱子，而且提升到相同高度，所以做功相同。功率取决于时间；A只用10秒，B用15秒，因此A的功率较大。

Bahasa Melayu

Kerja yang dilakukan adalah sama kerana kedua-dua pekerja mengangkat kotak yang sama ke ketinggian yang sama. Kuasa bergantung pada masa. Pekerja A mengambil 10 s, manakala pekerja B mengambil 15 s.

(c) Calculate work done

Given: m = 20 kg g = 10 m s⁻² h = 15 m Work done = mgh = 20 × 10 × 15 = 3000 J
口诀：
Work up = mgh. 向上提升做功 = m × g × h。 Kerja mengangkat = mgh.
Same mass + same height = same work.
同质量 + 同高度 = 同做功。 Jisim sama + tinggi sama = kerja sama.
Shorter time = greater power. 时间越短，功率越大。 Masa lebih singkat, kuasa lebih besar.
