3. Human circulatory system

1. A = vena cava; B = aorta.
2. Vessel A has lower blood pressure and lower oxygen concentration than vessel B.
3. During running, the heart pumps more blood because more oxygen is needed; vessel B carries a greater volume of blood and blood flow increases.
4. The left ventricle has a thicker muscular wall than the right ventricle to produce the pumping force needed to pump blood to the whole body.

5. Shoots responding to light

1. To investigate the response of plants towards light.
2. The shoot grows towards the direction of the hole.
3. Phototropism.
4. If the hole is closed, shoot growth becomes stunted because it cannot receive light; photosynthesis cannot be carried out properly and plant growth is affected.
