B1 Homeostasis organs

Which organs are involved in maintaining homeostasis?

Answer: Lungs / Peparu; Kidney / Ginjal; Skin / Kulit; Pancreas / Pankreas
English: Homeostasis means maintaining a stable internal environment. Lungs regulate carbon dioxide, kidneys regulate water and salts and remove urea, skin regulates body temperature, and the pancreas regulates blood glucose.
华语：稳态就是维持身体内部环境稳定。肺调节二氧化碳；肾脏调节水分和盐并排出尿素；皮肤调节体温；胰脏调节血糖。
Bahasa Melayu: Homeostasis ialah pengekalan persekitaran dalaman yang stabil. Peparu mengawal karbon dioksida, ginjal mengawal air dan garam serta menyingkirkan urea, kulit mengawal suhu badan, dan pankreas mengawal glukosa darah.
Steps: 1) Identify the function of each organ. 2) Ask whether it regulates an internal condition. 3) Select organs that help keep conditions stable.
Keywords: homeostasis = stable internal environment / 内环境稳定 / persekitaran dalaman stabil

B2 Batteries and bulb brightness

Original answers: Series connection; brightness increases as more batteries are added; supplied relationship states increases then decreases; six 2 V batteries in parallel → No.
English: In series, the voltages of batteries add together, increasing potential difference across the bulb. In parallel, identical 2 V batteries still provide about 2 V across the bulb, so six batteries do not become 12 V.
华语：电池串联时，总电压会相加，所以灯泡通常会更亮。相同的 2 V 电池并联时，电压仍约为 2 V，不会因为有六粒就变成 12 V。
Bahasa Melayu: Dalam sambungan bersiri, voltan bateri dijumlahkan dan beza keupayaan pada mentol meningkat. Dalam sambungan selari, bateri 2 V masih membekalkan kira-kira 2 V, bukannya 12 V.
Steps: Series → add voltage. Parallel → voltage stays approximately the same for identical cells.
Keywords: series = voltage adds / 串联电压相加 / bersiri = voltan bertambah

B3 Sea breeze

Answers: Area Y; sea water changes temperature less than land; Sea breeze / Bayu laut; Convection / Perolakan
English: During daytime, land heats up faster than the sea. Warm air above land rises. Cooler, denser air above the sea moves toward land to replace it. This movement of air is sea breeze and the heat transfer mechanism is convection.
华语：白天陆地升温比海水快。陆地上方的空气受热上升，海面上方较冷、密度较大的空气便吹向陆地补充，因此形成海风。空气循环属于对流。
Bahasa Melayu: Pada waktu siang, daratan memanas lebih cepat daripada laut. Udara panas di atas daratan naik, lalu udara yang lebih sejuk dan tumpat dari laut bergerak ke darat. Fenomena ini ialah bayu laut dan berlaku melalui perolakan.
Steps: Land heats faster → air above land rises → cool sea air moves to land → sea breeze.
Keywords: land heats faster → sea breeze → convection

B4 Spring

Answer: Elastic potential energy / 弹性势能 / Tenaga keupayaan kenyal
English: A stretched or compressed spring is deformed, so energy is stored as elastic potential energy. The supplied answer accepts these methods to make the spring stiffer: smaller spring diameter, larger wire diameter, and shorter spring.
华语：弹簧被拉长或压缩时发生形变，所以储存弹性势能。原答案接受的“让弹簧更硬”方法包括：弹簧直径较小、线径较大、弹簧较短。
Bahasa Melayu: Spring yang diregang atau dimampatkan mengalami perubahan bentuk dan menyimpan tenaga keupayaan kenyal. Jawapan asal menerima: diameter spring lebih kecil, diameter dawai lebih besar dan spring lebih pendek.
Steps: Look for deformation. If an elastic object is stretched/compressed, think elastic potential energy.
Keywords: stretched/compressed → elastic potential energy

B5 Satellite information sequence

Answer: 2 → 1 → 4 → 3
English: Follow the signal path: the ground transmitter sends the signal to the satellite, the satellite receives/processes it, then retransmits it to Earth, where the receiving station receives it.
华语：追踪讯号路线：地面发射站发送讯号 → 卫星接收/处理 → 卫星把讯号传回地球 → 地面接收站收到讯号，因此顺序是 2-1-4-3。
Bahasa Melayu: Ikut laluan isyarat: stesen pemancar di Bumi menghantar isyarat → satelit menerima/memproses → satelit menghantar semula ke Bumi → stesen penerima menerima. Maka urutannya 2-1-4-3.
Keyword: transmitter → satellite → Earth receiver

C1 Reproductive system

1) B = Prostate gland. It secretes a milky white fluid that activates or increases sperm mobility.
English: Similarities: sperm and ovum are reproductive cells, and production of both involves cell division. Differences: sperm is produced by testes and has a tail; ovum is produced by ovaries, has no tail and is round. Physical changes in a teenage girl given in the key: acne; development of breasts and hips.
华语：相同点：精子和卵细胞都是生殖细胞，两者的产生都涉及细胞分裂。不同点：精子由睾丸产生而且有尾巴；卵细胞由卵巢产生、没有尾巴且呈圆形。女生青春期的身体变化：长青春痘、乳房和臀部发育。
Bahasa Melayu: Persamaan: sperma dan ovum ialah sel pembiakan dan penghasilan kedua-duanya melibatkan pembahagian sel. Perbezaan: sperma dihasilkan oleh testis dan berekor; ovum dihasilkan oleh ovari, tidak berekor dan berbentuk bulat. Perubahan fizikal remaja perempuan: pertumbuhan jerawat serta perkembangan buah dada dan pinggul.
Steps: For compare-and-contrast questions, separate marks into similarities and differences. Compare place of production and structure.
Keywords: prostate gland → milky fluid → sperm mobility

C2 White blood cells and immunity

Answers: White blood cells; produce antibodies; ingest and digest pathogens with enzymes.
English: White blood cells protect the body by producing antibodies and by engulfing and digesting pathogens. Immunity is the ability of the body system to fight pathogens before disease develops. Types listed in the supplied answer: naturally acquired active, artificially acquired active, naturally acquired passive, artificially acquired passive immunity.
华语：白血球通过制造抗体，以及吞噬并用酶消化病原体来保护身体。免疫是身体对抗病原体的能力。原答案列出四种：天然主动、人工主动、天然被动、人工被动免疫。
Bahasa Melayu: Sel darah putih melindungi badan dengan menghasilkan antibodi serta menelan dan mencerna patogen menggunakan enzim. Keimunan ialah keupayaan sistem badan melawan patogen. Jenis keimunan dalam skema: aktif semula jadi, aktif buatan, pasif semula jadi dan pasif buatan.
Steps: Active immunity = body produces antibodies. Passive immunity = antibodies are received from another source.
Keywords: antibody + phagocytosis / 抗体 + 吞噬 / antibodi + fagositosis

C3 Heating and cooling water

Answers: 10 minutes; liquid → gas; CD has less water than AB because some water became steam during BC; 300 ml takes longer; salt water maximum slightly above 100°C and minimum slightly below 0°C.
English: A flat region at boiling means temperature remains constant while liquid changes to gas. Some water escapes as steam, so the remaining volume decreases. A larger volume needs more heat energy. Dissolved salt raises boiling point and lowers freezing point.
华语：沸腾平台表示温度保持不变，但水正在由液态变成气态。部分水变成蒸气离开，因此剩余体积较少。水量越大，需要吸收的热能越多。加盐会提高沸点并降低冰点。
Bahasa Melayu: Bahagian mendatar semasa mendidih menunjukkan suhu kekal tetapi air berubah daripada cecair kepada gas. Sebahagian air menjadi wap, jadi isipadu berkurang. Isipadu air yang lebih besar memerlukan lebih banyak tenaga haba. Garam menaikkan takat didih dan menurunkan takat beku.
Steps: Read graph → identify state change → compare amount of water → apply boiling/freezing point effect of salt.
Keywords: plateau = change of state; salt → boiling point ↑, freezing point ↓

C4 Eye and vision

Answers: Ciliary muscle changes lens thickness; near object → ciliary muscle contracts and lens becomes thicker/more curved; eyeball too long → short-sightedness; correction → concave lens.
English: For a near object, the ciliary muscles contract and the lens becomes thicker so it can refract light more strongly. A long eyeball causes light from distant objects to focus in front of the retina. A concave lens diverges light before it enters the eye so the final image forms on the retina.
华语：看近物时，睫状肌收缩，晶状体变厚、弯曲度增加，使光线折射更强。眼球过长时，远处光线会在视网膜前聚焦，形成近视。凹透镜先让光线发散，使影像重新落在视网膜上。
Bahasa Melayu: Untuk objek dekat, otot silia mengecut dan kanta menjadi lebih tebal serta lebih melengkung supaya pembiasan lebih kuat. Bola mata terlalu panjang menyebabkan cahaya objek jauh difokuskan di hadapan retina. Kanta cekung mencapahkan cahaya supaya imej akhir terbentuk pada retina.
Steps: Near object → ciliary muscle contracts → lens thick. Long eyeball → image in front of retina → short-sightedness → concave lens.
Keywords: long eyeball → short-sightedness → concave lens

C5 Cellular respiration and photosynthesis

Word equation: Oxygen + Glucose → Energy + Carbon dioxide + Water
English: Cellular respiration uses oxygen and glucose to release energy and produces carbon dioxide and water. It is not the same as breathing. Breathing is the mechanical movement of air into and out of respiratory organs. The supplied comparison states that photosynthesis depends on sunlight, uses water and carbon dioxide, produces glucose and oxygen, and occurs in chloroplasts; cellular respiration uses glucose and oxygen, produces water, carbon dioxide and energy, and occurs in mitochondria.
华语：细胞呼吸利用氧气和葡萄糖释放能量，并产生二氧化碳和水。它和“呼吸动作”不同，呼吸动作只是空气进出呼吸器官的机械过程。原答案比较：光合作用依赖阳光，利用水和二氧化碳，产生葡萄糖和氧气，发生在叶绿体；细胞呼吸利用葡萄糖和氧气，产生水、二氧化碳和能量，发生在线粒体。
Bahasa Melayu: Respirasi sel menggunakan oksigen dan glukosa untuk membebaskan tenaga serta menghasilkan karbon dioksida dan air. Ia berbeza daripada pernafasan, iaitu proses mekanikal keluar masuk udara. Mengikut skema, fotosintesis bergantung pada cahaya matahari, menggunakan air dan karbon dioksida, menghasilkan glukosa dan oksigen serta berlaku dalam kloroplas; respirasi sel menggunakan glukosa dan oksigen, menghasilkan air, karbon dioksida dan tenaga serta berlaku dalam mitokondria.
Steps: Memorise reactants and products, then compare location and sunlight dependence.
Keywords: respiration → mitochondria; photosynthesis → chloroplast

C6 Radioactivity — Iodine-131

Iodine-131 decays to a more stable xenon atom by emitting a beta particle.
English: Half-life is the time taken for the number of undecayed nuclei to become half the original number. Iodine-131 has a half-life of 8 days. Over 24 days: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 half-lives. The present amount is 1.25 million nuclei. Work backwards by doubling three times: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei. Beta particles can penetrate paper but not aluminium or lead because their penetrating power is moderate. Beta particles are negatively charged because they are fast-moving high-energy electrons.
华语：半衰期是未衰变原子核减少到原来一半所需的时间。Iodine-131 的半衰期是 8 天，24 ÷ 8 = 3 个半衰期。现在剩 1.25 million，反推过去每次乘 2：1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 million nuclei。β 粒子可穿过纸，但不能穿过铝和铅，因为穿透力中等。β 带负电，因为它本质上是高速、高能电子。
Bahasa Melayu: Separuh hayat ialah masa yang diambil untuk bilangan nukleus yang belum mereput berkurang kepada separuh bilangan asal. Separuh hayat Iodine-131 ialah 8 hari. Dalam 24 hari: 24 ÷ 8 = 3 separuh hayat. Bilangan sekarang ialah 1.25 juta. Kira songsang dengan mendarab 2 tiga kali: 1.25 → 2.5 → 5 → 10 juta nukleus. Zarah beta menembusi kertas tetapi bukan aluminium atau plumbum kerana kuasa penembusannya sederhana. Beta bercas negatif kerana merupakan elektron bertenaga tinggi yang bergerak pantas.
Steps: Number of half-lives = total time ÷ half-life. Forward in time → ÷2 each half-life. Work backwards → ×2 each half-life.
Keywords: 24 ÷ 8 = 3; backwards = ×2; beta = electron, negative, moderate penetration
