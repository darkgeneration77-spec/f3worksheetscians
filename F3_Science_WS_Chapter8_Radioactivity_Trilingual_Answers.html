8.2 — Atom and Nucleus

Concept Explanation

EnglishA neutral atom has equal numbers of protons and electrons. Losing electrons forms a positive ion; gaining electrons forms a negative ion.
华语中性原子：质子数 = 电子数。失去电子形成正离子；得到电子形成负离子。
Bahasa MelayuAtom neutral mempunyai bilangan proton sama dengan elektron. Kehilangan elektron membentuk ion positif; menerima elektron membentuk ion negatif.
protonelectronneutronpositive ionnegative ioncharge
口诀: 失电子正，得电子负。 Lose e− = positive; gain e− = negative.
Count protons and electrons.
If equal, total charge = 0.
Electron lost → positive ion.
Electron gained → negative ion.

8.3 — Ionising and Non-ionising Radiation

Concept Explanation

EnglishCompare alpha, beta and gamma using charge, ionising power, penetrating power and deflection.
华语比较 α、β、γ 时抓四点：电荷、电离能力、穿透能力、偏转方向。
Bahasa MelayuBandingkan alfa, beta dan gama melalui cas, kuasa pengionan, kuasa penembusan dan pemesongan.
alphabetagammaionising powerpenetrating powerdeflectionbackground radiationGeiger counter
口诀: α 正、强电离、弱穿透；β 负、中等；γ 中性、弱电离、强穿透。
Check the charge first.
Then compare ionising power.
Next compare penetrating power.
Finally use deflection direction to confirm.
